LUNAR PHASES & MARKET CHARACTER:

  ● NEW MOON (0-1 days)      → High Volatility, Reversals
                                 Action: Use tight stops, watch for flips

  ● WAXING MOON (1-14 days)  → Rising Energy, Bullish
                                 Action: Favor LONGS, hold winners, add on dips

  ● FULL MOON (14-16 days)   → Peak Energy, Profit-Taking
                                 Action: Take profits, consider SHORTS

  ● WANING MOON (16-29 days) → Declining Energy, Bearish
                                 Action: Favor SHORTS, reduce long positions

================================================================================

MARKET INTENSITY SCALE (Trading Volatility Forecast):

  ❌ 0-20:   Dead market (rare)
  🔵 20-40:  Low volatility   → Range-bound, lower average move
  🟢 40-70:  Normal volatility → Standard conditions
  🟠 70-85:  High volatility  → REDUCE position size, tight stops
  🔴 85-100: Extreme volatility → Minimal size, watch for gaps

================================================================================

OPTIMAL TRADING WINDOWS (IST):

  Entry:  Sunrise + 15 minutes (allows market participants to assess overnight news)
  Exit:   Sunset - 15 minutes (reduces after-hours risk)

  Example (Mumbai):
    Sunrise: 6:00 AM  →  Optimal Entry: 6:15 AM
    Sunset:  6:00 PM  →  Optimal Exit:  5:45 PM

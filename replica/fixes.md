## 3. What is unsolved

- **Did my screen work?** Screens are point-in-time snapshots. There is no
  backtest and no record of what a screen's picks did afterwards ("what i still
  see, screener lacks is backtesting over a few year horizon"
  [#1458](https://forum.valuepickr.com/t/screener-in-the-destination-for-intelligent-screening-reporting-in-india/1148/1458)).
  Thin: 1 post, but the add-on market and the Trendlyne/FidelFolio replies
  around it point the same way.
- **The fundamentals-plus-technicals investor.** Screener's own users send
  technical requests elsewhere: "Screener is more suited for fundamental
  screens"
  [#1464](https://forum.valuepickr.com/t/screener-in-the-destination-for-intelligent-screening-reporting-in-india/1148/1464).
  The 24 technical requests come from people who want both in one place.
- **Trust in the number.** The top two complaints together (39 posts) say users
  check Screener's numbers by hand because they can't see the formula or its
  provenance.

## 5. The angle

The skill asks for three positioning lines. For a personal desk they work as
design principles:

1. **Every number shows its working.** For investors who don't trust a ratio
   whose formula they can't see, the desk shows the formula, the inputs and the
   date beside each number. Evidence: opacity plus data errors, 39 posts across
   2 sources.
2. **Fundamentals and technicals on one page.** For investors who are told to
   "use Chartink for that", the desk keeps both together. Evidence:
   technicals, 24 posts across 2 sources.
3. **Did the screen work?** For investors whose screens are snapshots, the desk
   records what each pick did afterwards (rec_audit). Evidence: thin, 1 post
   plus the add-on and backtest threads.

**Recommended: #1.** It extends the desk's strongest existing habit (the
measured vs hypothesis labels, claims_lint) and answers the largest complaint
in the sample.

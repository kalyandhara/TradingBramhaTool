### Section 1: LIQUIDITY & INDUCEMENT

**Definition text:**
> "Markets move from one pool of liquidity to another. Inducement is a trap that lures retail traders early, which institutions then sweep."

**Diagram elements:**
- BSL (Buy Side Liquidity) — labeled above a swing high
- SSL (Sell Side Liquidity) — labeled below a swing low
- "Inducement" arrow pointing upward toward BSL (a false breakout above BSL that sweeps retail long stops before a reversal down)
- Price action shown as a series of candles sweeping BSL, then reversing back down toward SSL

**Key idea:** Liquidity pools sit above swing highs (BSL = resting buy stops from retail shorts) and below swing lows (SSL = resting sell stops from retail longs). Institutions induce retail entries at extremes, sweep the stops, then reverse.

---

### Section 2: MARKET STRUCTURE SHIFT (MSS)

**Definition text:**
> "When price breaks a short-term swing level with displacement, signaling a true change in trend bias and institutional participation."

**Diagram elements:**
- Downtrend shown with lower highs and lower lows
- BOS (Break of Structure) labels on lower low breaks (continuation of trend)
- CHoCH (Change of Character) label at the point where price finally breaks a prior swing high with a strong displacement candle
- A large bullish displacement candle breaks the last CHoCH level → MSS confirmed

**Key idea:** MSS = BOS + displacement candle. Regular BOS without displacement is just continuation. CHoCH is the first sign a trend is ending. MSS requires an aggressive, wide-range candle proving institutional buyers (not just retail) entered.

---

### Section 3: FAIR VALUE GAPS (FVG) & OTE

**Definition text:**
> "Displacement is highly reliable when it causes a Break of Structure (BOS) or Change of Character (CHoCH), proving institutional participation."

**Diagram elements:**
- FVG zone highlighted between candle 1 high and candle 3 low (standard 3-candle FVG structure)
- OTE Zone (Optimal Trade Entry) marked at the 61.8%–79% Fibonacci retracement of the displacement leg
- Price pulls back into the FVG/OTE zone after the BOS displacement
- Entry arrow at the OTE zone

**Key idea:** After a displacement-caused BOS, price often retraces into the resulting FVG. The OTE zone (61.8–79% fib of the displacement leg) is the highest-probability entry area — it aligns with where the FVG sits and where institutional orders were left unfilled.

---

### Section 4: EXECUTION BLUEPRINT

**Step sequence:**
1. Spot MSS (BOS or CHoCH with displacement)
2. Wait for FVG pullback in the OTE zone (61.8–79% retracement)
3. Enter with stop loss **above the displacement high** (for shorts) or below the displacement low (for longs)
4. Target the **opposite liquidity pool** (SSL if long, BSL if short)

**Diagram elements:**
- Full trade setup shown: BOS displacement candle → FVG zone → OTE zone (shaded) → entry candle inside OTE → Stop Loss marked above the displacement high → Target at the opposing liquidity pool
- Labels: BOS, FVG, OTE Zone, Stop Loss, Target (opposite pool)

## Concepts Identified

1. **BSL / SSL** — Buy Side Liquidity / Sell Side Liquidity (swing high/low stop clusters)
2. **Inducement** — False breakout designed to sweep retail stops before institutional reversal
3. **MSS** — Market Structure Shift; requires displacement (wide-range institutional candle)
4. **BOS** — Break of Structure (trend continuation break)
5. **CHoCH** — Change of Character (first counter-trend structure break)
6. **Displacement** — A qualifying condition: the break candle must be wide-range and aggressive, not just a close
7. **FVG** — Fair Value Gap (3-candle imbalance from a displacement move)
8. **OTE** — Optimal Trade Entry zone (61.8–79% Fibonacci retracement into the FVG)
9. **Execution Blueprint** — The 4-step ICT trade sequence: MSS → FVG/OTE → Entry → Target opposite pool

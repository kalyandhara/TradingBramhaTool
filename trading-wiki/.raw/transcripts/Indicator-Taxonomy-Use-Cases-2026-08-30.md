### 1. Why Use Indicators? (Google-Maps Analogy)
* Indicator ~ Google Maps — the road (price) does not change because of the map, but the map makes navigation easier and more confident.
* Purpose: give probability clues, reduce emotional noise, help decide when and where to enter, exit and manage.
* Not a magic box: no indicator predicts price with certainty; they show probabilities feeding a complete system.

### 2. Lagging vs Leading
* Lagging (MA, EMA, Bollinger, RSI, MACD, ADX): built from historical price, show what already happened.
* Leading (Ichimoku, pivot-based tools, some volatility bands): claim to anticipate; still derived from past data.
* Claim: both can be profitable in the right market context with price-action confirmation.

### 3. Six families
Trend-following (MA, SuperTrend, Parabolic SAR, EMA band) · Momentum/oscillators (RSI,
Stochastic, TSI, RVI) · Reversal/divergence (RSI/MFI divergence, MACD histogram turn, SAR
flip) · Volume (MFI, OBV, Volume Profile) · Volatility (ATR, Vortex, ADX, Keltner,
Donchian) · Composite (Ichimoku, MAMA, EMA band).

### 4. Use-case matrix
| Market condition | Indicators claimed to work best |
|---|---|
| Strong trending | Parabolic SAR, EMA band, SuperTrend, MAs, Vortex (VI+ > VI−) |
| Breakout / momentum burst | EMA band broadening, SAR flip, Vortex, MFI volume spike |
| Sideways / range | Stochastic, RSI, MFI OB/OS zones, ATR stops, Bollinger squeeze |
| Low-volatility consolidation | no strong indicator — price action, or option-selling |
| High-volatility / news | Vortex, ADX, MFI, ATR-flexible stops |
| Multi-timeframe | HTF trend indicator + LTF entry oscillator |

### 5. Confluence
Pick 2–3 core tools; require alignment of trend + oscillator + volume; more than 4–5
indicators produces conflicting signals.

### 6. Myths debunked
Leading indicators do not truly lead · more indicators ≠ more accuracy · no indicator works
in every condition · no 100% predictive indicator · higher win-rate ≠ better system (RRR,
drawdown, consistency matter more).

### 7. Testing metrics
Win-rate, RRR, max drawdown, Sharpe/Sortino, stability across timeframes and assets.

### 9–12. Workflow, mindset, takeaways
Price action → add one trend indicator + one oscillator → require confluence → add a
volume/volatility filter → backtest the combo → iterate → move to multi-timeframe.
Risk 1–2% per trade, target 2–3R, process over prediction.
"Beginner's best combo": 50-EMA + RSI + ATR/MFI.

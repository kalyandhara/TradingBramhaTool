# Madhur Raut

## Profile
Retail-facing Indian trader / social-media educator. Publishes a daily **Market Sentiment Index** infographic — a composite 0-100 gauge summarising NIFTY breadth, options positioning, and technical trend. States "Not SEBI registered" — educational content only.

## What He Does
- Daily composite sentiment card ([[sources/Madhur-Raut-Market-Sentiment-Index-Infographic]]).
- Weighting emphasises **breadth** (PCR 30 + A/D 20 + Sectors 20 = 70) over price technicals (30).

## Notes & Observations
- Delivery format is polished, gauge-first — a strong reference point for our own top-of-dashboard sentiment card. See [[analyses/Market-Sentiment-Index-Roadmap-Fit-2026-07-14]].
- No visible track record / backtest — the card is descriptive, not predictive. Treat as a **reading**, not a signal.

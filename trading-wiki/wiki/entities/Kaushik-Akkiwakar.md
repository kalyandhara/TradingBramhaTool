# Kaushik Akkiwakar

SEBI-registered research analyst. Runs a YouTube channel covering trading and investing concepts from first principles. Deploys momentum-based swing/intraday setups at the [[entities/MarkUp-Platform]].

## Known Views

- **TSI advocacy** — argues [[concepts/True-Strength-Index-TSI]] is undervalued vs RSI; uses TSI(25,13,13) with [[concepts/SuperTrend]] / MAST for trend context. Reads TSI conditional on trend via a 4-quadrant matrix (see [[sources/YT-Kaushik-Akkiwakar-TSI-Momentum]]).
- **Point-and-Figure preference** — teaches TSI signals on [[concepts/Point-and-Figure-Charts]] to remove time-noise.
- **4-step stock selection** — market cap → volume → volatility → trend alignment. Applied on daily and 5-min timeframes with automated alerts on negative TSI crossovers.

# Zeiierman

## What
TradingView Pine Script author with **Wizard** status. Publishes open-source indicators with an emphasis on advanced visualization and signal processing (DSP-style filters). Runs zeiierman.com and a Discord trading community.

## Why it matters here
Another open-source indicator author whose scripts serve as *reference implementations* — same role as [[entities/LuxAlgo]] and [[entities/DGT]]. Zeiierman's distinguishing habit is bringing **signal-processing techniques** (e.g. a two-pole Butterworth low-pass filter for trend classification) and **volume-effort visualization** into otherwise-standard SMC primitives, which is what makes his FVG script contribute ideas the LuxAlgo/DGT scripts don't.

## Contributions ingested
- [[sources/FVG-Zeiierman-MTF-Pine]] (2026-07-22) — "Multitimeframe Fair Value Gap (FVG)"; source for the **Bull/Bear Power** in-gap volume-effort visualization and the **Butterworth trend-alignment filter** added to [[concepts/Fair-Value-Gap]], plus a wick-sensitivity impulse filter and HTF-or-LTF timeframe selection.

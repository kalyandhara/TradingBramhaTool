# MarkUp Platform

Trading-alert platform where [[Kaushik-Akkiwakar]] deploys his momentum-based system for live client alerts. Signals combine [[True-Strength-Index-TSI]] crossovers with a 4-step stock-selection filter (market cap → volume → volatility → trend alignment) and [[SuperTrend]] / MAST trend context, run on daily and 5-minute timeframes.

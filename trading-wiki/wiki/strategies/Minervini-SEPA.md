# Minervini SEPA Strategy

## Core Idea

**SEPA** (Specific Entry Point Analysis) is Mark Minervini's complete growth stock trading framework, developed over 33+ years. The goal is **superperformance** — annual returns of 40–100%+ — achieved by:

1. Buying only stocks in **Stage 2** uptrends (confirmed via Trend Template)
2. Entering at the optimal **VCP pivot point** with precise timing
3. Managing risk with hard stop-losses set before entry
4. Sizing positions for **1.25–2.5% equity risk** per trade
5. Selling into strength at **base count** and climax signals

Core principle: *Make big money when correct; avoid big drawdowns when wrong.*

## Setup Criteria (Pre-Trade Filter)

### Step 1 — Stage 2 Confirmation (Trend Template, all 8 required)

1. Price > 150-day AND 200-day MA
2. 150-day MA > 200-day MA
3. 200-day MA trending up ≥1 month (prefer 4–5 months)
4. 50-day MA > 150-day MA > 200-day MA (full MA stack in order)
5. Price ≥25% above 52-week low
6. Price within 25% of 52-week high (closer = better)
7. RS rank ≥70 (prefer 90s); RS line uptrending ≥6 weeks
8. Price above 50-day MA as stock emerges from base

**If any criterion fails → skip the stock.**

### Step 2 — Chart Pattern (VCP)

Look for a **Volatility Contraction Pattern** in the current consolidation:
- 2–6 successive pullbacks from left to right, each ~50% of the prior
- Volume contracts during each successive pullback (especially on the right)
- At least one day of extremely dry volume near pivot (below 50-day avg volume)
- **VCP footprint notation**: `WW depth/final T_count` → e.g., `27W 28/6 3T`

Valid base patterns within Stage 2:
- **Classic VCP / Cup-with-handle** (most reliable)
- **3-C pattern (Cup Completion Cheat)**: earlier entry; plateau 5–10% wide
- **Low Cheat**: entry in lower third; riskier, higher potential; useful for large caps and recent IPOs
- **Double Bottom**: W-shape with undercut preferred; add cheat/handle on right side
- **Power Play (High Tight Flag)**: stock up ≥100% in ≤8 weeks → tight flag ≤20% over 3–6 weeks; no fundamental requirement

## Entry Rules

- Buy at the **pivot buy point** — where price moves above the high of the tightest contraction on **expanding volume** (volume surge vs. recent dry volume)
- Enter as close to pivot as possible; do not chase more than 2–3% above pivot
- Confirm follow-through: multiple up days in first week or two; stock should be "hard to buy"
- **Do not buy**: falling stocks, Stage 4, stocks with multiple violations after a prior breakout

## Exit Rules

### Stop-Loss (Loss Protection)

- Set stop before entry; execute without question when hit
- **Maximum stop**: 10%; avg target 5–6%
- Position size must be adjusted so stop = 1.25–2.5% of total account equity
- Do not widen stops in volatile markets — tighten them
- After stock rises 3× initial risk: move stop to at least breakeven

### Profit-Taking (Systematic Sell Rules)

**Trailing mechanism:**
- Once 50-day MA catches up to breakeven cost → switch to 50-day as stop (Breakeven-or-Better Rule)
- **Back stop**: set at avg gain level (e.g., 10% floor) once stock is up 2× avg gain
- **Sell half rule**: if at 2× avg gain and uncertain → sell half; psychological win/win

**Sell into strength signals (late-stage):**
- Base count ≥5 → sell into breakouts; do not buy
- P/E expansion ≥2× from base 1 AND late-stage base → start selling
- Climax top: stock up 25–50%+ in 1–3 weeks
- 70%+ up days in 7–15 day window (late-stage exhaustion, not early-stage strength)
- Largest up day since move began AND/OR widest daily spread
- Exhaustion gaps

**Sell into weakness signals:**
- Largest down day since Stage 2 advance began (almost always outright sell)
- Down on heaviest volume since move began = institutional distribution
- Churning: high volume with little price progress
- Multiple violations: 3+ lower lows, close below 20-day soon after breakout, bad closes on expanding volume

## Timeframe

- **Primary**: Swing to position (weeks to months)
- **Hold criteria**: hold as long as stock is in Stage 2, no major violations, and reward/risk still favorable
- **Turnover**: not taboo — move capital to best opportunities; compound edge frequently

## Position Sizing for Optimal Results

| Metric | Guideline |
|--------|-----------|
| Risk per trade (% of equity) | 1.25–2.5% |
| Max stop-loss | 10% |
| Avg loss target | ≤5–6% |
| Optimal position size | 20–25% |
| Max position size | 50% (exceptional) |
| Portfolio stocks | 4–8 (small); 10–12 (large) |

**Scaling approach:**
- Pilot buy: start at quarter position; add as evidence confirms
- **Add and Reduce**: buy more on pullback to new base within uptrend; raise stop on entire position to level where risk stays constant
- **Two-for-One Rule**: sell half each of two underperformers → fund one full new position

## Best Market Conditions

- Early to mid bull market (Stage 2 leaders proliferating)
- Market off a bottom: buy leaders breaking out first (leaders bottom first, before general market)
- "Lockout rally": market ignores overbought readings in early bull — this is a sign of strength, not a warning
- Look for expansion in new 52-week high list vs. new 52-week low list

## Worst Market Conditions

- Bear market (Stage 4 dominates; serial gappers common)
- High volatility / choppy market: reduce position sizes and tighten stops
- When your batting average drops or avg gain shrinks: de-risk immediately; reduce exposure

## The Eight Keys to Superperformance

### Keys to Generating Big Performance
1. **Timing**: buy at the line of least resistance (VCP pivot); time the market via charts not opinions
2. **Concentration**: 4–12 best names; "di-worsification" kills alpha
3. **Turnover**: move capital to best opportunities; don't hold dead money
4. **Maintain Risk/Reward**: re-evaluate every day; short-term view on losses, longer-term on gains

### Keys to Limiting Drawdowns
1. **Sell into strength**: sell when buyers are plentiful; 12 × 10% compounds better than 1 × 40%
2. **Trade small before big**: scale up on wins; scale down or stop on losses
3. **Trade directionally**: never buy falling stocks; wait for the turn
4. **Protect breakeven**: once at a decent profit, move stop up aggressively

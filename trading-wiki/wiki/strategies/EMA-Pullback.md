# EMA Pullback Strategy

## Core Idea
Enter when price pulls back to a rising EMA in an established trend,
using RSI to time the entry at the pullback low.

## Setup Criteria
- Clear uptrend on daily (HH/HL structure)
- 21 EMA sloping upward
- Price pulls back to 21–50 EMA zone
- RSI at 40–50 on daily (healthy correction, not crashed)

## Entry Rules
- Bullish candle closing back above EMA after pullback
- Confirm: decreasing volume on pullback, expanding on entry candle

## Exit Rules
- **Stop:** Below the pullback swing low
- **Target 1:** Prior swing high (take partial profit)
- **Target 2:** Trail stop on remainder, 1.5–2x risk

## Best Conditions
- Trending bull market, low VIX

## Worst Conditions
- Choppy flat market, high VIX, around earnings

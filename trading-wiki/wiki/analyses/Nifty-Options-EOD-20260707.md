# Analysis: Nifty Options — EOD Brief 2026-07-07

## Thesis
Nifty spent 2026-07-07 pinned toward the **24,400 max-pain magnet** and closed **24,353.7 (-0.37% from open 24,444.55)**, sitting **at the session low (0% of session range 24,353.7–24,526.45)**. The end-of-day options tape is characterised by **broad two-sided unwinding** — largest OI-change-since-open on both the 24,400 CE (short buildup, +2.58 Cr) and 24,400 PE (long unwinding, +2.84 Cr) — with the last 30 min showing aggressive unwinding above (24,500 CE −4.96 Cr) and short-covering on the ITM put side (24,450 PE −4.67 Cr). PCR **0.98** is neutral. **Read:** intraday distributive close into support with no directional conviction from either side — writers dominating at 24,400, longs on both wings folding into expiry proximity.

## Evidence For (bearish / range-bound expiry pin)
- **Spot at session low.** 0% of range 24,353.7–24,526.45; intraday trend path Up → Down → Down.
- **24,350 CE (ATM) is a short buildup.** New call writers layering resistance at spot; every up-move being sold.
- **24,400 CE carries highest call OI at 3,79,55,850 (OTM)** and added the most since open (+2,58,06,375, short buildup) → sellers defending 24,400 as the ceiling.
- **Max pain at 24,400** ≈ spot −0.19% below; expiry gravity centred just above spot.
- **Last-30-min flow:** 24,400 CE volume +1.27 Cr, 24,400 PE volume +2.13 Cr — put-side volume dominant, spot dropped −0.35% in the same window.
- **Aggregate outer OTM CE OI change +7,00,00,425 (short buildup)** — call writers stacking above; no roll-up conviction from bulls.

## Evidence Against (dip / bullish setup)
- **PCR 0.98** — sentiment is neutral, not extended bearish; no capitulation on the put side.
- **24,350 PE (ATM) is long unwinding.** Existing put longs are exiting; if they were pressing the downside they'd be adding, not folding.
- **24,450 PE last-30-min OI change −4,67,97,000 (short covering)** — ITM put writers *covering* into the drop is a defensive tell that a floor is being set, not that it's caving.
- **ITM CE (24,350) long unwinding (−31,94,100)** is small in absolute size — no aggressive call-long liquidation.
- **24,500 CE last-30-min OI change −4,96,53,975 (long unwinding)** — the ceiling isn't being reinforced, it's being *removed*; that's ambiguous, not bearish.

## Conclusion
**End-of-day read: range-bound close pinned to 24,400 max-pain, with a mild negative tape.** The dominant structural signal is call writers defending 24,400 (highest OI + biggest OI add since open), but the put-side is not confirming a directional break — PCR neutral, ATM PE long unwinding, ITM PE short-covering. Base case for the next session:

- **Upside gate: 24,400 → 24,450 → 24,500.** 24,400 is the writer wall; a 15-min close and hold above 24,450 with rising CE OI unwinding above would signal wall failure.
- **Downside gate: 24,350 → 24,300.** 24,350 ATM CE short buildup is the pivot; sustained hold below 24,350 with fresh PE long buildup (not seen today) would open the 24,300 handle.
- **Neutral base case (highest weight):** oscillation between 24,350 and 24,450 pinned by 24,400 max-pain until expiry positioning unwinds.

## Detailed Notes

### Market Breadth Summary
```
Nifty 50:   13 / 50 advances → 26% breadth    (-0.46%)
Nifty 500: 199 / 497 advances → 40% breadth   (-0.26%)
Nifty Bank:  4 / 13 advances  → 31% breadth   (-0.42%)
```
The Nifty 500 breadth (40%) is meaningfully better than Nifty 50 (26%), confirming that broader mid/small-cap market held up better than large caps.

### Crude Oil Sector Surge
The Strait of Hormuz narrative drove a single-day supply shock trade:
- **CPCL (Chennai Petroleum Corp)**: +15.0%, ₹1,426.10 — all-time high; Oil refinery, direct beneficiary of crude price spike as refining margins widen when crude surges faster than product prices lag
- **MRPL (Mangalore Refinery)**: +11.2%, ₹181.14 — volume 17.99× average; secondary refinery play

> [!key-insight] Both crude oil names hit extreme volume multiples on the same geopolitical driver — a classic sector-rotation-on-macro-event pattern, not a stock-specific story.

### 52W Highs — Notable
- **Finolex Cables (FINOLEXCAB)**: ₹1,207.85, volume 25.4× — strongest volume signal of the day. Electricals sector. Unrelated to crude narrative.
- **Gland Pharma (GLAND)**: ₹2,924.40 (+9.6%) — Healthcare, 52W high. Large-cap pharma strength.
- **Nifty Next 50**: ₹74,650.85 — index level, signals mid-cap outperformance.
- **BSE Consumer Durables**: ₹65,629.51 — sector index, consumer discretionary strength.

### 52W Lows — Notable
- **KEC International**: ₹450.75 (-5.3%) — Power/infrastructure, bucking the market's energy-sector rally
- **JK Lakshmi Cement**: ₹549.25 — Cement sector weakness persists

### India VIX Anomaly
India VIX -3.1% on a day NIFTY fell -0.46%. Normally VIX rises on market declines. Possible readings:
1. Options expiry dynamics compressing IV
2. Market participants not hedging → complacency risk
3. The decline is orderly (low realized volatility), consistent with slow distribution

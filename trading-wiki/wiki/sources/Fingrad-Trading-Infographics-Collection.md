- Two **multi-indicator confluence** setups worth recording: *Supertrend + 5/20 EMA crossover* and *Supertrend + RSI + ADX*.

- The rest (Bollinger squeeze, "trend is your friend" EMA, price-action-with-MACD, 5-8-13 & 50/200 crosses, confirmation checklist, trader taxonomy) largely restates knowledge already covered better in existing pages — used as cross-reference/source links, not new pages.

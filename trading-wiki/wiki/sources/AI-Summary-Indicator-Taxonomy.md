# AI summary — indicator taxonomy & use-cases

Supplied by the owner alongside a request for a coverage gap analysis. A six-family
taxonomy of indicators (trend, momentum, reversal, volume, volatility, composite), a
market-condition → indicator routing table, a myths section, and a settings cheat-sheet.

> [!warning] Lowest provenance tier in this wiki
> This is **not a transcript** — it is another model's summary of a video whose speaker,
> channel, URL and date were never supplied. Nothing in it is attributable, so nothing in
> it can be checked against what was actually said. It contains no backtest, no sample
> size, and no losing example. It is filed as a source only so that
> [[concepts/Indicator-Selection-By-Regime]] has something to point at.

## What was kept

**One idea:** regime should determine which indicator family is allowed to signal. Promoted
to [[concepts/Indicator-Selection-By-Regime]] with an explicit UNVALIDATED status and a
named test.

## What was not kept, and why

- **The six-family taxonomy** — already covered page-by-page ([[concepts/RSI]],
  [[concepts/ADX]], [[concepts/Stochastic-Oscillator]], [[concepts/True-Strength-Index-TSI]],
  [[concepts/KST]], [[concepts/CCI]], [[concepts/Volume-Profile]], [[concepts/SuperTrend]],
  [[concepts/VWAP]], [[concepts/Volume-Analysis]]). Restating it adds a page, not knowledge.
- **The lagging-vs-leading section** — self-refuting as written: it concedes leading
  indicators are also derived from past data, which dissolves the distinction it opens with.
- **The over/under-rated nominations** (KST over-rated; MFI, Vortex, MAMA under-rated) —
  asserted with no evidence. Recorded in the raw file, not promoted.
- **The settings cheat-sheet** — default parameters, available from any reference.
- **The risk/mindset sections** — duplicate [[concepts/Capital-Management-Methods]],
  [[concepts/Kelly-Criterion]] and [[concepts/Performance-Metrics]], which are better sourced.

## Where it fed the gap analysis

Its indicator list was used as one input to [[analyses/Indicator-Coverage-Gap]] — as a
*checklist of names to test coverage against*, not as evidence any of them work. The gap
analysis reaches a conclusion the source does not: the tool's real gap is the **aggressor
side of order flow** ([[concepts/Order-Flow]]), which this source never mentions.

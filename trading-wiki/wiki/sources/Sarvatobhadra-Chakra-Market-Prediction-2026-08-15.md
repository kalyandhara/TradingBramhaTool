## Key Takeaways

1. **Sarvatobhadra Chakra (SBC) is a nakshatra-based Vedic grid** that assigns planet-nakshatra "veda" (aspect) relationships to market sectors. Majority malefic vedas = bearish day; majority benefic = bullish. On the day of recording: 7 malefic out of 9 → market confirmed highly bearish.

2. **Three veda types per planet**: Sanmukha (front), Vama (left/back), Dakshina (right/south). Each planet can simultaneously cast all three. The grid updates in real-time as planets transit nakshatras.

3. **Nama Nakshatra is language-agnostic**: the vowel sounds of a market's name ("Chandi" or "Silver") map to the same nakshatra, giving identical interpretations. Grounded in Sanskrit phonetics / mantra vibration theory.

4. **Reversal windows are the claimed unique innovation**: specific 10–20 minute intraday windows where market reversals are most probable. Examples from the recording day were shown to match: Gold/Silver top at 6:58–7:18, BTC 1000-point crash at 22:58–23:18, MCX Silver reversal at 8:32–8:45.

5. **Quick-use method requires no SBC knowledge**: check Panchang tithi → check SBC cell color ratio → note reversal window times → reduce size at windows. Suitable for intraday and swing traders.

6. **Panchang is the first filter**: if the day's tithi is inauspicious (e.g., Bhadra period, Krishna Ashtami), avoid buy-side entries regardless of technical signals. This integrates classical Panchang into a pre-market checklist.

7. **Jupiter retrograde pattern**: when Jupiter is retrograde, banking, auto, and FMCG sectors tend to outperform — claimed observation from the presenter, consistent with the India Independence Chart / Jupiter Crash Thesis already in wiki.

## Detailed Notes

### SBC Grid Mechanics

The classic SBC is a 9×9 grid of 81 squares, each corresponding to a nakshatra or vowel sound. Planets are placed in the grid based on their current nakshatra. Veda lines (front, back, left, right) are drawn from each planet's square and wherever they cross another nakshatra square, that nakshatra receives a "veda" from that planet.

| Veda Type | Direction | Meaning |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Agra / Sanmukha | Forward | Direct aspect, strongest |
| Vama | Left/backward | Indirect, moderate |
| Dakshina | Right/south | Oblique, situational |

Malefic planets (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu, Sun) casting vedas on a nakshatra = pressure/stress on that sector. Benefic planets (Jupiter, Venus, Moon, Mercury) = support.

### Sector Scoring System

Each sector gets a score (% bullish) based on veda count:
- **>60% green**: bullish bias for that sector
- **~50%**: neutral / consolidation expected
- **<40% green**: bearish bias

The scoring updates live as nakshatras transit.

## Contradictions with Existing Wiki

> [!note] No direct contradictions found
> The Vedic malefic/benefic planet interpretation here (malefics = bearish, benefics = bullish) **contradicts** [[concepts/Vedic-Planet-Trading-Aspects]], which states "Malefics (Saturn/Sun/Rahu/Mars) = bullish; Benefics (Jupiter/Moon/Mercury/Venus) = bearish" in the stock market context.
>
> **Resolution**: The two interpretations apply at different levels. The [[concepts/Vedic-Planet-Trading-Aspects]] rule (malefics = bullish for stocks) applies to karaka planet aspects on a stock's birth chart. SBC veda interpretation (malefics = bearish) applies to the market's nakshatra grid on a given day. They operate on different frames — individual stock vs macro market. NOT a true contradiction; document context-dependence.

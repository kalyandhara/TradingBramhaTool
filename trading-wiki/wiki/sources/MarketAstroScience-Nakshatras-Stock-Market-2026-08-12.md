# MarketAstroScience — 27 Nakshatras in the Stock Market

## Key Takeaways

1. **Framework is psychology-first, not sector-first.** Each nakshatra describes the *type of trader/approach* that is aligned with its energy — intraday scalper vs patient value investor vs contrarian bottomer — rather than "buy Pharma on this day."

2. **Guna (Sattva/Rajas/Tamas) as market condition proxy.** 9 Sattva nakshatras = clear analytical conditions; 9 Rajas = active/volatile momentum conditions; 9 Tamas = heavy, slow or chaotic market energy. Tamas days warrant caution or contrarian positioning.

3. **Trading timeframe mapping is complete for all 27.** Intraday (Ashwini, Hasta, Ardra, Ashlesha, Dhanishtha), Swing (Mrigashira, Vishakha, Purva Ashadha, Revati), Position (Magha, Punarvasu, Anuradha, Chitra), Long-term/SIP (Bharani, Rohini, Pushya, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada).

4. **Ashwini → Crypto & High Volatility** — the commodity tradition (screenshot source) says Pharma/Auto/Metal; this source says intraday momentum/crypto. Both are consistent with Ketu's explosive/spike energy, different time horizons.

5. **Purva Ashadha adds EV/Green Energy explicitly** — first source to tie a nakshatra to clean energy/EV themes. Relevant given NSE's new EV sector index.

6. **Shatabhisha is explicitly healthcare/pharma + derivatives** — confirms our sector mapping and adds the contrarian derivative-trading overlay.

7. **Tamas nakshatras (10 total) are not "bad" — they are contrarian:** Ardra, Ashlesha, Magha, Chitra, Mula, Dhanishtha, Shatabhisha, Purva Bhadrapada, Uttara Bhadrapada, and Jyeshtha (Rajas but contrarian by nature).

## Contradictions with Existing Wiki

> [!note] Framework difference, not factual contradiction
> The [[concepts/Nakshatra-Sector-Map]] was built from commodity-tradition sources (commodity associations by nakshatra from Vedic tradition). This source uses psychological/trading-style framing. **Ashwini** is the clearest divergence: commodity map says Pharma/Auto/Metal; this source says Crypto/Intraday momentum. Both are valid — different time horizons and different frameworks (commodity affinity vs lunar energy psychology). The wiki should hold both, not choose one.

## Key Takeaways
- **Core claim:** When Mercury turns **direct** (ends retrograde), NIFTY tends to **rally** (author quotes +6% to +27%, once +48% in 2026); during **retrograde** the index **stalls / falls / chops**, often with **fake breakouts and fake breakdowns**.
- **Transition dates as levels:** the exact retrograde→direct (and direct→retrograde) dates are claimed to act as **support/resistance** — mark that day's candle high & low and trade the break.
- **Mechanism offered:** none scientific — Mercury as "closest planet" influencing market *psychology*. Presented as pattern observation, not causation.
- **This is one factor among many** (author's own caveat): macro, earnings, policy also drive price.

## Recurrent Patterns (author's framing)
1. **Direct Mercury → strong rally** — +6% to +27% within days-to-weeks, often breaking the low of the retrograde-phase range.
2. **Retrograde Mercury → weakness / sideways / volatility** — with deceptive fake breakouts & breakdowns.
3. **Transition dates → S/R** — mark the candle's high/low on the status-change date; trade the break, watch for the fakeout first.

## Actionable Insights (as stated — treat as hypotheses to test, not rules)
- Chart the Mercury retrograde/direct transition dates; mark that candle's high & low.
- Expect **fake breakouts/breakdowns** right after a transition while price is still inside the retrograde range.
- Use the marked levels for stops/entries **only in combination with conventional technical/fundamental analysis**.

## Contradictions / Skeptical Flags
> [!contradiction] The 2026 "+48%" claim looks inflated / unverifiable
> As of this ingest (2026-07-23) NIFTY trades ~23,870 (per the dashboard's live index read). A +48% (~9,500-pt) advance after 21 Mar 2026 is not reflected in the current index level — treat that row as unreliable, likely a mis-read or projection rather than a realised move.

- **Sample size is tiny** (~14 direct-turns over 6 years) and the outcomes are **mixed in sign** (some direct-turns fall −6% to −12%), which undercuts the headline "direct → rally" rule the video leads with.
- **Post-hoc pattern-fitting risk:** "fake breakout/breakdown" is invoked whenever price *doesn't* follow the rule — an unfalsifiable escape hatch.
- **Confounds:** 2020 rallies coincide with the COVID-crash recovery; attributing them to Mercury ignores the dominant macro driver.
- **Not scientific / not advice** — the author says so; this wiki treats it accordingly (see [[concepts/Mercury-Retrograde-Market-Cycle]]).

## Key Takeaways
- Restates the standard basing/markup/distribution/markdown 4-phase cycle and HH/HL uptrend-structure rules — substantively the same content already documented in this wiki's [[concepts/Stage-Analysis]] (Weinstein's 4 stages) and [[concepts/Support-Resistance]] (swing-point marking). No new mechanics here; filed for two specific quotable framings worth keeping rather than for new technical content.
- **Useful framing #1 — scanners are research assistants, not decision-makers**: "Think of scanners as interns that fetch candidates—you still interview each chart." A clean, citable articulation of how to use any of the SwingEdge screener outputs already ingested (KDDL, NRBBEARING, AADHARHFC, INDSWFTLAB, SIGMA) — the scanner narrows the list, the trader still does the actual diligence.
- **Useful framing #2 — explicit caution on RS tools, directly relevant to the INDSWFTLAB tension already noted in this wiki**: "Tools like Swing Edge surface RS-style context; use it to narrow a list, not to skip your own chart read." This is the source's own stated philosophy behind why RS-100 can mean "leader" on one ticker and "overowned, chase risk" on another (see [[sources/SwingEdge-INDSWFTLAB-20260621]]) — RS is explicitly framed as a *filter*, not a standalone verdict, which resolves that earlier-noted tension rather than leaving it as an unexplained inconsistency.
- **"Failed breakdowns... can precede sharp squeezes; treat them as context, not automatic entries."** This is the same false-break-then-reclaim pattern already documented in [[strategies/False-Break-Buildup-Trading]] (trapped-trader fade) and in [[concepts/Distribution-Days]]'s "failed retest" pattern from the Downside Risk screener ingest — now confirmed as a recurring, cross-referenced idea across three independent SwingEdge/wiki sources.
- Explicit discipline note worth keeping: "If your thesis was solely 'it crossed a line,' you will be hurt often; add a second factor (volume, structure of the base, group strength)" — a concrete single-factor-trading anti-pattern warning.
- Confirms market/sector/correlation checking as "a cheap filter," not superstition — consistent with this wiki's existing sector-rotation tooling rationale (checking sector context before individual-stock entries).

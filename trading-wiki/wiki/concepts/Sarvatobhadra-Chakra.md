# Sarvatobhadra Chakra (SBC)

## Definition

**Sarvatobhadra Chakra** is a classical Vedic astrology grid attributed to Lord Shiva, traditionally used for timing auspicious and inauspicious events. The chakra is a 9×9 matrix of 81 squares arranged so that the 27 nakshatras (lunar mansions) and their associated vowel sounds (vowels of Sanskrit) occupy specific cells. Planets are placed in the grid at their current nakshatra position, and "veda" (aspect) lines radiate in four directions — front, back, left, right — crossing other nakshatra cells and creating aspect relationships.

In financial astrology, SBC is used to:
1. Determine the overall market bias for a given day (bullish/bearish/neutral)
2. Identify which sectors will be favored or pressured
3. Pinpoint intraday reversal time windows

---

## How It Works

### The 9×9 Grid

The SBC grid places nakshatras and vowel sounds at fixed positions. The four cardinal directions from each planet's cell define "veda lines." Where a veda line crosses another nakshatra cell, that nakshatra receives a veda (aspect) from that planet.

### Veda Types (Three per Planet)

| Veda | Sanskrit Name | Direction | Strength |
|------|--------------|-----------|---------|
| Front/Forward | Agra / Sanmukha | Facing direction of planet's motion | Strongest |
| Left/Back | Vama | Counter to motion | Moderate |
| Right/South | Dakshina | Perpendicular right | Situational |

A planet can cast all three vedas simultaneously — each crossing a different set of nakshatra cells.

### Malefic vs Benefic Vedas

| Planet | Nature | Market Impact When Casting Veda |
|--------|--------|--------------------------------|
| Saturn | Malefic | Bearish pressure on that nakshatra's sector |
| Mars | Malefic | Bearish, aggressive moves |
| Rahu | Malefic | Erratic, sudden bearish moves |
| Ketu | Malefic | Bearish, exit signal |
| Sun | Malefic (context-dependent) | Bearish when with malefics |
| Jupiter | Benefic | Bullish support |
| Venus | Benefic | Bullish (commodities, luxury) |
| Moon | Benefic (changes fast) | Short-term bullish |
| Mercury | Benefic | Bullish (financial, communication sectors) |

**Scoring**: count total malefic vedas vs benefic vedas across all cells active that day.
- Majority malefic (>5/9): bearish day
- Majority benefic: bullish day
- Balanced: choppy / sector-rotation day

## Nama Nakshatra — Sound-Based Sector Mapping

A unique feature of SBC is that each market/commodity/sector has a "nama nakshatra" (name nakshatra) derived from the vowel sounds of its name. This is rooted in Sanskrit phonetics and mantra theory — each Sanskrit vowel vibrates at a specific frequency corresponding to a nakshatra.

**Key property**: Language-agnostic. "Chandi" (Hindi for silver) and "Silver" (English) map to the same nakshatra through their phonetic vowel sounds, producing identical SBC interpretations.

**Practical use**: instead of knowing which nakshatra corresponds to "silver," simply look up the sector's nama nakshatra and check what vedas are crossing that cell today.

## Reversal Windows

The most novel application in modern financial SBC is the **reversal window** concept — specific 10–20 minute intraday time slots where a planetary nakshatra transit creates a veda "switch point," making a market reversal most probable.

### How Reversal Windows Are Calculated

When a fast-moving planet (Moon primarily; also Sun, Mercury for swing windows) crosses from one nakshatra's cell boundary into the next, the vedas it was casting shift. This transition moment is the reversal window.

### Reversal Window Properties

- **Duration**: typically 10–20 minutes (fast-moving planets) to 2–6 days (slow planets for swing/positional)
- **Tolerance**: ±10 minutes for intraday windows
- **Rule**: if market makes a high or low INSIDE the reversal window, expect that high/low to hold for the rest of the session (or the swing period)
- **Size management**: reduce position size ±10 min around the window; let the reversal confirm before adding

### Reversal Window Types

| Type | Planet Basis | Timeframe |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Intraday | Moon transit | Same day |
| Swing | Sun/Mercury transit | 2–6 days |
| Positional | Mars/Jupiter transit | Weeks |
| Monthly | Slow planets | Month |
| Yearly | Saturn/Rahu | Year |

## Quick-Use Workflow (No SBC Expertise Required)

```
Morning pre-market:
1. Check Panchang → is today's tithi auspicious?
   - Bhadra active → avoid buy entries
   - Inauspicious tithi (Krishna Ashtami, etc.) → sell-side or no trade
2. Open SBC dashboard → count green vs red cells
   - >60% green: bullish bias
   - >60% red: bearish bias
   - Mixed: chop expected, wait for sector clarity
3. Note reversal window times for your session
4. Select your sector → check its nama nakshatra veda → confirm alignment with overall bias
5. Check hora for current time → bullish/bearish hora for your product

During session:
- Reduce size ±10 min around reversal windows
- If market makes a swing high/low at reversal window → treat it as the session anchor
- Do not force counter-trend trades against the dominant veda color
```

> [!note] Context-Dependent Malefic Interpretation
> [[concepts/Vedic-Planet-Trading-Aspects]] states malefic planets (Saturn/Mars/Rahu) = bullish for stocks when aspecting a stock's natal chart. SBC says malefic vedas = bearish for the market day.
>

> - Vedic Planet Aspects: natal karaka planet transiting a stock's sensitive degree → that stock moves bullish
> - SBC vedas: macro market grid for the day → day's directional bias
> Always apply both in context: a stock might move up (natal aspect) on a bearish day (SBC malefic dominance) if its karaka is being aspected beneficially.

## Common Mistakes

- **Ignoring Panchang**: SBC signals should always be filtered through the day's tithi/vara first. A bullish SBC on an inauspicious Bhadra day is unreliable.
- **Applying English-only nama nakshatras**: use the phonetic vowel analysis, not just literal translation.
- **Missing the tolerance window**: reversal windows are ±10 min, not exact to the second. Waiting for a confirmed reversal candle within the window is better than pre-empting it.
- **Over-weighting one planet**: SBC is a composite score. One malefic planet doesn't make a bearish day if 6 other planets are casting benefic vedas.
- **Manual static SBC charts**: traditional manual SBC doesn't update in real time, making intraday reversal windows impossible to calculate. Only dynamic software handles this reliably.

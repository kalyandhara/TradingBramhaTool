# India Independence Chart — Financial Astrology Trading Framework

## Core Premise

India's **Independence Chart** (Aug 15, 1947, 12:00 AM, New Delhi) is used as the **national horoscope** — the base chart against which all Indian market analysis is conducted. When transiting planets hit sensitive degrees in this chart, market moves follow for Indian indices and stocks.

This is **Layer 1 only** — the simplest version. Advanced layers add nakshatra padas, kakshya divisions, multiple divisional charts, and multi-planet synthesis.

## Company Analysis — Name-Based Rashi Assignment

Every Indian company can be assigned a **Rashi (zodiac sign)** and **Nakshatra** based on the first syllable of its name, using the Chandra Rashi (Moon sign) naming convention from Vedic astrology.

### Step-by-Step Method

1. **Extract the first syllable** of the company name
2. **Identify the Nakshatra pada** that syllable maps to
3. **Derive the Rashi** (each nakshatra's four padas span one sign)
4. **Identify lords**:
   - **Nakshatra Lord** (ruling planet of the nakshatra)
   - **Rashi Lord** (ruling planet of the sign)
5. **Identify Karaka planets** for the company's business sectors (see [[concepts/Vedic-Planet-Trading-Aspects]])
6. **Track transits** of these lords and karakas against India's D1/D9 sensitive degrees

## Trading Logic — The Three-Layer Signal

### Layer 1: Degree Matching (taught in course)

**Rule:** A transit is significant when a **karaka planet** hits a **sensitive India chart degree**.

```
Signal = Karaka Planet × Sensitive Degree × Correct Sign
```

**Process:**
1. Open a chart for the date of interest at 9:15 AM IST (NSE open)
2. Check transiting planets' degrees
3. Match against India's D1/D9 degree table (above)
4. Confirm: is the transiting planet a karaka for the stock?
5. Check: does the planet aspect the stock's Rashi?

**Bullish confirmation stack (all should align):**
- [ ] Karaka planet hits India D1 or D9 sensitive degree
- [ ] Planet is in bullish mode (Saturn/Sun/Rahu/Mars unafflicted)
- [ ] Planet aspects the stock's Rashi
- [ ] Nakshatra lord or Rashi lord is also activated (conjunct or aspected)
- [ ] Dispositor chain intact (planet sits in a sign whose lord is also active)

### Layer 2+ (advanced — not yet documented)
- Nakshatra pada analysis (which of the 4 padas the planet is in)
- Kakshya (sub-sub-divisions of nakshatras)
- Multiple divisional charts (D10 for career/business, D16 for vehicles, etc.)
- Multi-planet synthesis (when 3+ planets hit different sensitive degrees simultaneously)

**Context:** Major Reliance rally started Oct 6, 2021.

**Result:** Multiple confirmations (degree + aspect + dispositor + nakshatra lord all active) → high-confidence bullish → major rally from Oct 6 onward.

## Bullish vs Bearish Planet Classification

From the Financial Astrology Course (Layer 1 framework):

| Classification | Planets | Logic |
|---------------|---------|-------|
| **Bullish (market-rising)** | Saturn, Sun, Rahu | Malefics drive fear-based rallies; institutional buying |
| **Bearish (correction risk)** | Jupiter, Moon, Mercury, Venus | Benefics drive euphoria → correction follows |
| **Explosive / Ambiguous** | Mars, Ketu | Direction depends on aspect context; Mars = momentum (up or down) |

> [!contradiction] Contradicts intuitive reading
> This framework is **opposite** to the popular "Jupiter = good, Saturn = bad" view. The reasoning:
> markets rise through greed and fear management (malefic discipline), and top out when
> benefic optimism peaks. Both frameworks can be correct depending on what "good for market" means:
> Jupiter produces economic expansion but price correction; Saturn produces price rallies amid restriction.

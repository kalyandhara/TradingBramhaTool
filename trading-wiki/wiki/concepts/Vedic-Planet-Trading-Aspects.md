# Vedic Planet Trading Aspects

## Core Framework

In Vedic (Jyotish) astrology, every planet casts a **full aspect (drishti)** on the 7th house from its position (180°). Three planets cast **additional special aspects**. These aspects are used in financial astrology to determine which planet is "activating" a sensitive degree — the activation being bullish or bearish depending on the planet's nature and the framework applied.

> [!note] Trading Bias Convention (Financial Astrology Course)
> Counterintuitively, **malefic planets = bullish** for markets, **benefic planets = bearish**.
> The logic: markets rise through struggle, fear, and scarcity (malefic energy) and correct when
> euphoria/optimism (benefic energy) peaks. This is the convention used in
> [[concepts/India-Independence-Chart-Trading]]. The absolute-bullish/bearish tags below
> follow this convention.

## Aspect House Reference

Vedic aspects are counted **inclusively** from the planet's house position:

| Planet | Houses Aspected (from its position) | Degrees (approx.) |
|--------|-------------------------------------|-------------------|
| **All planets** | 7th house | 180° |
| **Mars only** | 4th, 7th, 8th | 90°, 180°, 210° |
| **Jupiter only** | 5th, 7th, 9th | 120°, 180°, 240° |
| **Saturn only** | 3rd, 7th, 10th | 60°, 180°, 270° |
| **Rahu/Ketu** | 5th, 7th, 9th | 120°, 180°, 240° |

> [!note] Orb for degree-matching (financial astrology)
> When checking if a transiting planet is "hitting" a sensitive natal degree:
> - **Exact degree (±0°)**: strongest signal
> - **±1°**: strong
> - **±2°**: moderate; use for confirmation only
> - Beyond 2°: generally not counted for precise degree-matching work

## Shashtashtak Yoga (6th/8th Axis)

Used in the Jupiter Crash Signal. If Planet A is in sign X, Planet B is in the **6th sign forward** (X+5 signs) or **8th sign forward** (X+7 signs) from A, they form Shashtashtak — a malefic tension aspect associated with sudden disruption.

| Position | Count | Direction |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| 6th from | +5 signs forward | 150° |
| 8th from | +7 signs forward | 210° |

This is **sign-based** (not degree-based), so the orb is the entire sign.

## Benefic vs Malefic — Natural Classification

| Classification | Planets | Trading Implication |
|---------------|---------|---------------------|
| **Natural Benefics** | Jupiter, Venus, Mercury (waxing), Moon (waxing, bright) | Bearish bias in financial astrology framework; market correction risk when strong |
| **Natural Malefics** | Saturn, Sun, Mars, Rahu, Ketu | Bullish bias; market rally fuel when strong and unafflicted |
| **Neutral / Context-Dependent** | Mercury (waning), Moon (waning) | Flip with context |

> [!note] Contextual override
> A planet's trading bias shifts if it is:
> - **Afflicted** (conjunct strong malefic, in enemy sign, combust the Sun) → weakened signal
> - **Exalted / Own sign** → strengthened signal
> - **Forming Yoga** (Gajakesari, Laxmi, Raj Yoga) → benefic yogas can override bearish bias and produce rallies even in corrective planetary periods

## Planet Dispositor Chain

In the degree-matching technique, the **dispositor** (sign lord of where a planet sits) creates a chain of influence:

```
Transiting Planet → Sign Lord (dispositor) → Nakshatra Lord → Business Karaka
```

All three legs of the chain being activated simultaneously = strongest signal.

**Example (Reliance, Oct 2021):**
- Saturn (karaka for oil/gas) at 12° Capricorn → matches India D9 Lagna 12°
- Saturn is in Capricorn (own sign — no dispositor chain leak)
- Saturn aspects Libra (Reliance's Rashi) via 3rd house aspect
- Mars (Chitra lord, Reliance's nakshatra lord) conjunct Sun/Moon/Mercury
- Venus (Libra lord, Reliance's Rashi lord) disposited by Mars (activated)
- **All chain links active → high-confidence bullish signal**

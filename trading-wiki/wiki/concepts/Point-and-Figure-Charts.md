# Point-and-Figure Charts (P&F)

Time-independent chart type. Columns of Xs (rising) and Os (falling) plotted by price movement, not by time. A new box is added only when price moves by the *box size*; a new column starts only when price reverses by the *reversal amount* (typically 3 boxes).

## Why It Matters

- Removes time-based noise — chop and gaps compress; real support/resistance stands out.
- Trend classification is unambiguous — column type alone tells you direction.
- Pattern vocabulary (Turtle, Triple Top/Bottom, Catapult, Broadening) maps to explicit box arithmetic.

## Standard Patterns Mentioned in TSI Context

- **Turtle Pattern** — consolidation-breakout signal on P&F.
- **Broadening Top** — high-volatility churning pattern; needs momentum confirmation.
- **Double Bottom Sell** — reversal signal after prior uptrend exhaustion.
